# Agent Atlas — Which AI tool for which job.

A directory of AI tools and agent use cases, ranked for builders. Every tool is a real product you can go use today.

Source: https://agent-atlas.pages.dev/for-ai.html | Full machine index: https://agent-atlas.pages.dev/llms.txt
Last verified: 2026-10-15. 79 tools, 12 use cases.

## Vibe coding & app building
Tools that turn a plain-English description into working software.
- **Cursor** (https://cursor.com/) — The AI-first code editor most professional builders end up in. Deep repo awareness and multi-file edits. Best for: Existing codebases, pro developers. Price: Free tier, Pro ~$20/mo.
- **Claude Code** (https://claude.ai/code) — Terminal agent that reads your repo, runs commands and edits many files. Strongest on large, messy codebases. Best for: Big refactors and architecture work. Price: With Claude Pro/Max.
- **GitHub Copilot** (https://github.com/features/copilot) — The low-friction entry point. Lives inside VS Code, JetBrains and Visual Studio. Best for: Teams on mixed editors. Price: Individual ~$10/mo.
- **Windsurf** (https://windsurf.com/) — Cursor's closest rival, with a proactive agent and a flat monthly price. Best for: Teams wanting predictable cost. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Replit** (https://replit.com/) — Browser-based build-and-host. You describe it, the agent writes it and deploys it. Best for: Fast prototypes you can ship. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Lovable** (https://lovable.dev/) — Full-stack app builder aimed at non-technical founders. Best for: Non-coders shipping a real app. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Bolt.new** (https://bolt.new/) — Prompt-to-full-stack in the browser with a generous free tier. Best for: Rapid prototyping. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **v0** (https://v0.app/) — Vercel's builder. Best balance of power and accessibility for non-technical builders. Best for: UI-first app generation. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Devin** (https://devin.ai/) — Autonomous software engineer that works through tickets on its own. Best for: Delegating whole tasks. Price: Team plans.
- **Cline** (https://cline.bot/) — Open-source coding agent that runs inside VS Code with your own API key. Best for: Bring-your-own-model control. Price: Free (you pay model usage).
- **Aider** (https://aider.chat/) — Terminal pair-programmer that edits files in your local git repo and commits. Best for: Git-native, scriptable workflows. Price: Free (you pay model usage).
- **Gemini CLI** (https://github.com/google-gemini/gemini-cli) — Google's open-source terminal agent with a large context window and a generous free tier. Best for: Google-ecosystem developers. Price: Free tier.
- **OpenAI Codex** (https://chatgpt.com/codex) — Runs coding tasks in parallel in isolated cloud environments. Included with ChatGPT plans. Best for: ChatGPT-first teams. Price: With ChatGPT Plus+.
- **Zed** (https://zed.dev/) — A very fast editor with AI built in, built in Rust. Best for: Speed and pair programming. Price: Free, paid AI plan.
- **Base44** (https://base44.com/) — Describe an app and get a working internal tool with a database attached. Best for: Internal business tools. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Rork** (https://rork.com/) — Vibe-coding for mobile — prompt a native phone app. Best for: iOS/Android prototypes. Price: Paid plans.

## AI agents & automation
Tools that let software do multi-step work on its own.
- **Zapier** (https://zapier.com/) — The widest connector library anywhere. If two apps exist, Zapier probably links them. Best for: Connecting business apps. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Make** (https://www.make.com/) — Visual scenario builder that handles complex branching far more cheaply than per-task pricing. Best for: Multi-step automations. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **n8n** (https://n8n.io/) — Open-source workflow automation you can self-host, with native AI agent nodes. Best for: Self-hosted, data-sensitive work. Price: Free self-hosted.
- **Lindy** (https://www.lindy.ai/) — Email and meeting agents that act like a virtual teammate. Best for: Inbox and scheduling agents. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Gumloop** (https://www.gumloop.com/) — Drag-and-drop AI workflows that scrape, enrich and write on a schedule. Best for: Data-in, content-out pipelines. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **CrewAI** (https://www.crewai.com/) — Open-source framework for teams of agents with defined roles. Best for: Developers building agent teams. Price: Free open source.
- **LangGraph** (https://www.langchain.com/langgraph) — Build stateful agents as graphs — the most controllable agent framework. Best for: Production agent engineering. Price: Free open source.
- **Manus** (https://manus.im/) — General-purpose agent that browses, researches and produces deliverables. Best for: Delegating research tasks. Price: Paid plans.
- **Relevance AI** (https://relevanceai.com/) — Build a workforce of AI agents with tools, then run them like staff. Best for: Multi-agent business ops. Price: Free tier, paid plans.

## Video generation & editing
From text to finished video, including avatars and auto-clipping.
- **Runway** (https://runwayml.com/) — The most complete generative video suite, from text-to-video to editing. Best for: Creative video production. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Sora** (https://sora.com/) — OpenAI's text-to-video generator with strong physics and coherence. Best for: Cinematic short clips. Price: With ChatGPT plans.
- **Google Veo** (https://deepmind.google/models/veo/) — Google's video model, strong on realistic motion and audio pairing. Best for: High-fidelity generation. Price: With Google AI plans.
- **HeyGen** (https://www.heygen.com/) — Talking-avatar videos from a script — the fastest route to a presenter video. Best for: Avatar/VSL videos. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Synthesia** (https://www.synthesia.io/) — Enterprise avatar video with team collaboration and brand kits. Best for: Training and corporate video. Price: Paid plans.
- **Descript** (https://www.descript.com/) — Edit video by editing the transcript. Removes filler words automatically. Best for: Podcasts and talking-head edits. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **OpusClip** (https://www.opus.pro/) — Turns one long video into a batch of vertical clips with captions. Best for: Repurposing to Shorts/Reels. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Pictory** (https://pictory.ai/) — Script or URL in, captioned video out, using stock footage. Best for: Faceless content at volume. Price: Paid plans.
- **CapCut** (https://www.capcut.com/) — Free editor with strong AI tools and captions, mobile and desktop. Best for: Social video editing. Price: Free, paid Pro.

## Voice & audio
Voice cloning, AI phone agents, and music generation.
- **ElevenLabs** (https://elevenlabs.io/) — The reference standard for lifelike AI voices and cloning. Best for: Narration and voice agents. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Deepgram** (https://deepgram.com/) — Fast, accurate speech-to-text and voice agent APIs built for developers. Best for: Transcription at scale. Price: Usage-based.
- **Vapi** (https://vapi.ai/) — Build and deploy AI phone agents that book appointments and qualify leads. Best for: Inbound/outbound call agents. Price: Usage-based.
- **Retell AI** (https://www.retellai.com/) — Low-latency voice agents with an easy testing and monitoring console. Best for: Production phone agents. Price: Usage-based.
- **Bland AI** (https://www.bland.ai/) — Phone agents focused on high-volume outbound calling. Best for: Outbound call campaigns. Price: Usage-based.
- **Suno** (https://suno.com/) — Full songs with vocals from a text prompt. Best for: Music beds and jingles. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Udio** (https://www.udio.com/) — High-fidelity AI music with fine control over style and structure. Best for: Music production. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Murf** (https://murf.ai/) — Studio-grade AI voiceover with a large voice library and fine pacing control. Best for: Narration and e-learning. Price: Free tier, paid plans.

## Image generation & design
Making visuals, UI and brand assets.
- **Midjourney** (https://www.midjourney.com/) — Still the most distinctive visual style in AI imagery. Best for: Artistic and editorial images. Price: Paid plans.
- **Ideogram** (https://ideogram.ai/) — The best free option when the image needs readable text in it. Best for: Logos, posters, typography. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Adobe Firefly** (https://www.adobe.com/products/firefly.html) — Commercially safe generation trained on licensed content, wired into Photoshop. Best for: Brand-safe commercial work. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Recraft** (https://www.recraft.ai/) — Vector-native generation — real SVG output for logos and icons. Best for: Brand and icon systems. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Gemini (Nano Banana)** (https://gemini.google.com/) — Google's image model, unusually good at editing an existing photo on request. Best for: Photo editing and iteration. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Figma** (https://www.figma.com/) — The default design tool, now with AI to draft layouts and fill content. Best for: UI and product design. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Gamma** (https://gamma.app/) — Decks and documents that generate from a prompt and look designed. Best for: Pitch decks and one-pagers. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Framer** (https://www.framer.com/) — Design and publish a site in one place, with AI to draft pages. Best for: Marketing sites. Price: Free tier, paid plans.

## Writing & thinking
Drafting, editing and reasoning with a model.
- **Claude** (https://claude.ai/) — The strongest general writing and analysis model for long documents. Best for: Long-form and nuanced work. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **ChatGPT** (https://chatgpt.com/) — The default general assistant, with image, voice, code and agent modes. Best for: Everything-in-one. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Grammarly** (https://www.grammarly.com/) — Line-level correction and tone control everywhere you type. Best for: Polishing existing writing. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Jasper** (https://www.jasper.ai/) — Marketing copy at scale, with brand voice and campaign workflows. Best for: Marketing teams. Price: Paid plans.
- **Copy.ai** (https://www.copy.ai/) — GTM workflows — prospecting, content and sales copy in one place. Best for: Sales and marketing ops. Price: Free tier, paid plans.

## Research & search
Finding answers with sources attached.
- **Perplexity** (https://www.perplexity.ai/) — Answers with citations you can check, which makes it usable for real research. Best for: Fact-finding with sources. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **NotebookLM** (https://notebooklm.google.com/) — Upload your own documents and question them. Grounded only in your sources. Best for: Understanding your own files. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Elicit** (https://elicit.com/) — Systematic literature review — screens papers and extracts findings into a table. Best for: Academic research. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Consensus** (https://consensus.app/) — Answers questions from peer-reviewed studies only. Best for: Evidence-based claims. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Exa** (https://exa.ai/) — Search built for AI agents — semantic web search as an API. Best for: Powering your own research agent. Price: Usage-based.

## Data & scraping
Getting clean data out of messy websites.
- **Firecrawl** (https://www.firecrawl.dev/) — Turns any website into clean markdown or structured JSON for your agent. Best for: Feeding sites into LLMs. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Apify** (https://apify.com/) — Thousands of ready-made scrapers plus a platform to run your own. Best for: Structured web data. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Browse AI** (https://www.browse.ai/) — Point at a page, train a robot to extract fields, run it on a schedule. Best for: No-code monitoring. Price: Free tier, paid plans.

## Sales & outreach
Finding, enriching and contacting prospects.
- **Apollo** (https://www.apollo.io/) — The largest B2B contact database with built-in sequencing. Best for: Finding and emailing prospects. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Clay** (https://www.clay.com/) — Enrich and combine 100+ data sources to build hyper-targeted lists. Best for: Personalisation at scale. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Instantly** (https://instantly.ai/) — Cold email sending with inbox warm-up and deliverability tooling. Best for: Cold email campaigns. Price: Paid plans.
- **Smartlead** (https://www.smartlead.ai/) — Unlimited mailboxes and warm-up, popular with agencies. Best for: Agency sending at volume. Price: Paid plans.
- **lemlist** (https://www.lemlist.com/) — Multichannel sequences across email and LinkedIn. Best for: Personalised multichannel. Price: Free tier, paid plans.

## Code review & quality
Agents that review pull requests before humans do.
- **CodeRabbit** (https://www.coderabbit.ai/) — Line-by-line PR review with suggested fixes, on every pull request. Best for: Catching issues pre-merge. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Greptile** (https://www.greptile.com/) — Reviews code with full-repo context, so it catches cross-file bugs. Best for: Large codebases. Price: Paid plans.
- **Qodo** (https://www.qodo.ai/) — AI test generation and review focused on correctness. Best for: Raising test coverage. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Snyk** (https://snyk.io/) — Finds and fixes security vulnerabilities in your dependencies and code. Best for: Security scanning. Price: Free tier, paid plans.

## Support & chat agents
Answering customers without a person typing.
- **Intercom Fin** (https://www.intercom.com/fin) — Support agent that resolves tickets from your own help content. Best for: Deflecting support volume. Price: Per resolution.
- **Chatbase** (https://www.chatbase.co/) — Train a chatbot on your docs and embed it in minutes. Best for: Docs-based support bots. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Voiceflow** (https://www.voiceflow.com/) — Design conversational agents for chat and voice in one canvas. Best for: Complex conversation design. Price: Free tier, paid plans.

## Meetings & notes
Recording, transcribing and summarising your calls.
- **Fathom** (https://fathom.video/) — Free meeting recorder that produces summaries and action items. Best for: Sales call notes. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Otter** (https://otter.ai/) — Live transcription and searchable meeting archive. Best for: Transcripts you can search. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Granola** (https://www.granola.ai/) — Takes your own rough notes and expands them using the meeting audio. Best for: People who take their own notes. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
- **Notion** (https://www.notion.com/) — Workspace with AI search and writing across everything you've stored. Best for: Knowledge base plus AI. Price: Free tier, paid plans.
